I work on computer vision — building methods for visual anomaly detection and for recognizing human action across changing domains.

I'm a doctoral researcher at Aalto University's School of Electrical Engineering (ELEC), advised by Prof. Yu Xiao. My research centers on industrial and video anomaly detection and on cross-domain few-shot action recognition, with ongoing collaborations in multimodal sensing and large language models for structured data.

Before Aalto, I worked as an engineer at Huawei's Chengdu Research Institute (2022–2023), and earned my master's degree at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (2019–2022). Feel free to reach out at changyi.li@aalto.fi.

Research
01

Visual Anomaly Detection

Detecting structural and logical defects in industrial images and anomalous events in video — through knowledge distillation, training-free calibrated fusion, and scene-adaptive temporal modeling.

02

Cross-Domain Few-Shot Action Recognition

Recognizing human activity when training and deployment domains differ, with a focus on transductive, training-free inference and on understanding what actually drives generalization.

03

LLMs & Multimodal Sensing

Collaborative work on large language models for querying domain-specific knowledge graphs (text-to-Cypher), and on multimodal sensing for beam prediction toward 6G.
